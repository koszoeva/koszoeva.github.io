INTRODUCTION

About me

I’m a senior technical writer and illustrator with 9 years of experience making complex software and telecom products easier to understand.

My main strengths are API documentation, user guides, technical visuals, and docs-as-code workflows using Git and CI/CD. I enjoy bringing structure to messy content and turning complicated topics into something clear and usable.

I enjoy learning new tools and staying current with emerging technologies. I use AI, scripting, and automation to streamline repetitive tasks, solve practical documentation challenges, and continuously improve the way I work.

Alongside my full-time work, I also contribute to AI training and language quality projects, helping improve multilingual LLM outputs and reviewing content for clarity, accuracy, and consistency.

Outside of tech, I also work internationally as a snooker referee. It has strengthened my focus, fairness, and calm decision-making under pressure.
