About me

I’m a senior technical writer and illustrator with 9 years of experience making complex software and telecom products easier to understand. My core strengths are API documentation, user guides, technical visuals, and docs-as-code workflows. I like bringing structure to messy content and turning complicated topics into something clear and usable.

I like learning new tools and staying current with emerging technologies. From improving workflows to creating custom AI scripts for repetitive tasks, I’m always looking for smarter ways to work.

Alongside my full-time work, I also contribute to AI training projects, helping improve multilingual LLM outputs and reviewing content for clarity, accuracy, and consistency. Outside of tech, I work as a professional snooker referee. It has strengthened my focus, fairness, and calm decision-making under pressure.
